Other languages

Information in other languages is under construction.

Tjuvmyndigheten is working to make its information available in all languages spoken in Sweden. In line with the agency’s work on equality and equal treatment, no language may be given priority over another. Information will therefore be published in all languages at the same time, or not at all.

At present, information is available in no languages other than Swedish. All other languages thus receive the same level of service, and the goal of equal treatment of languages is considered met.

An English version of the website was previously available on this page. It has been appropriated.
